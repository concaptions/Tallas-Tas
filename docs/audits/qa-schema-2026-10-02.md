## Method and what "verdict" means

For each of the 21 pinned tables I confirmed the table id against the live base, read the live field
list and types, found the Drizzle table through
`packages/db/src/scripts/import-mappings.ts` `TABLE_MAPPINGS[*].drizzleImport`, and resolved every
mapped `drizzleColumn` against the actual `pgTable` definition in `packages/db/src/schema/`. Column
lists were parsed from the schema files, not transcribed. Link verdicts were established by reading
every `.from()`, `.delete()` and `.insert()` site of every junction table across `packages/` and
`apps/`.

Airtable **computed** types (`formula`, `multipleLookupValues`, `rollup`, `count`, `createdTime`,
`lastModifiedTime`, `createdBy`, `lastModifiedBy`, `autoNumber`, `button`) are counted separately and
never as missing. Where the pinned list names one, it is flagged, because the app must **derive** it.

Read-only verification against the production database (`DATABASE_URL`, a Railway Postgres proxy) was
used only to confirm or refute specific claims; nothing was written. All 44 journal entries
(`0000`–`0043`) are applied in production, so every gap below is a schema-design gap, not an
unapplied migration.

## Per-table field parity

Legend for the verdict column: **OK** = stored in a column of a suitable type · **JUNCTION** = held
by the named junction table · **OTHER SIDE** = the relationship is stored, but on the inverse table ·
**MISSING** = nothing stores it · **COMPUTED** = Airtable-computed, must be derived · **LOSSY** = a
column exists but the type loses data.

## Two-way link status

A link is **TWO-WAY** only if one junction table holds it, both sides can read it, both sides can
write it, and it is registered in `packages/domain/src/links/link-kinds.ts` so the shared `LinkField`
can mount it. **ONE-WAY** means a single FK, or a junction whose read or write exists for one side
only. **ABSENT** means nothing stores the relationship.

The registry holds exactly 4 junctions and 8 kinds (`link-kinds.ts:11-12`, `:25-34`), and the generic
writer `packages/db/src/links.ts:39-68` knows the same 4. `apps/web/src/lib/link-actions.ts:81-95`
dispatches on the registry and revalidates both sides. All 8 kinds are mounted in the UI
(`products/product-panel.tsx:262`, `ugc/creator-panel.tsx:588`, `personas/persona-panel.tsx:211`,
`concepts/[conceptId]/concept-detail.tsx:532` and `:642`, `angles/angle-panel.tsx:628`, `:641`, `:656`).

### Cannot be added without a decision

1. **The angle/concept inversion.** Gratsi stores product and persona on the **concept** and shows
   them on the angle as lookups; our schema stores them on the **angle** and has no concept-level
   store. Adding `concept_products` / `concept_personas` is mechanical; deciding whether
   `angle_products` / `angle_personas` stay (as a derived convenience) or go (as a duplicate of the
   truth) is a product decision, and they are two of only four two-way links in the app.
2. **Which collapsed links become junctions.** Six relationships are stored as single `uuid` FKs
   where Airtable allows many. Each conversion is a migration plus a rewrite of every reader.
3. **Making the 24 one-way links two-way.** This is not a migration: 20 of the 24 already have a
   junction table. What is missing is the second-side `list`/`loadAll`/`sync` helper, a
   `LINK_REGISTRY` entry, and a `LinkField` mount. `LinkJunction` is a closed union of 4 strings in
   **two** places (`packages/domain/src/links/link-kinds.ts:11` and `packages/db/src/links.ts:18`),
   and `LinkTable` is a closed union of 5; extending the registry to the other 17 junctions means
   widening both unions and adding a `ownsSource` branch per new table in
   `apps/web/src/lib/link-actions.ts:46-58`. Worth its own ticket, and worth deciding whether the
   two unions should be one.
4. **Status option sets.** Eight of 13 live `Creative Design · Internal Status` options, two of 11
   `Creative Sheet · Internal Status`, two of six `Creative Sheet · Status`, three of six
   `Concepts · Production Status`, all three `Angles · Potential` and one
   `UGC Management · Partnership Activity` option have no key in our tuples. Several are clearly
   Gratsi-specific noise ("Sent to Feriel", "Sent to Nadish", a `Type` option literally named `s`).
   Which to adopt, which to translate and which to drop on migration is the owner's call, and it is
   not a schema change — these columns are plain `text`.
5. **`collections.angle_id` and `collections.product_id`.** Both are written from live fields that
   cannot supply them (`Angles` links Concepts; `(Internal) Product` is text). Either the importer
   stops trying and the columns join
   `DRIZZLE_COLUMNS_WITHOUT_AIRTABLE_SOURCE`, or `angle_id` is dropped in favour of reading through
   `concept_collections`. Verified 0/5 populated in production.
6. **Why `creative_modules` (35 live), `creative_sheet_items` (377 live) and `creator_products`
   (27 live links) hold 0 rows in production.** The importer has code for all three. This is a
   migration-run question, not a schema question, and I could not establish the cause from the code.

### Not a migration, but belongs on the same ticket

`creators.concept_ids` and `creators.product_ids` (jsonb) duplicate `creator_concepts` and
`creator_products`. Two stores for one relationship is the kind of drift the junctions were added to
remove.
